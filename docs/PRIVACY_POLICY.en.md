# UtilLock Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026.

> Before publishing, replace `[LEGAL NAME]`, `[PRIVACY EMAIL]`, and `[COUNTRY]`, then host this policy at a stable public URL.

`[LEGAL NAME]`, based in `[COUNTRY]`, controls UtilLock. Privacy requests may be sent to `[PRIVACY EMAIL]`.

Blocking rules, schedules, selected apps, and domains remain on the device. Accessibility locally processes the visible app package and, in supported browsers, the address bar; it does not store or send general screen content. Optional Usage Access locally checks the foreground app.

DNS queries are sent directly to Cloudflare DNS/Family for resolution or filtering; UtilLock does not retain DNS history. A challenge photo is temporarily cached. When cloud evaluation applies, it is sent through a UtilLock Supabase Edge Function to OpenAI, using `store:false`. The photo is not stored in Supabase and is deleted from device cache after evaluation. OpenAI may temporarily retain limited data for safety under its current policies.

Supabase stores a user ID, challenge assignment, attempts, outcome, quota, and timestamps. If Google is linked, Google identity/email data is processed. Purchase token, product, status, and expiry are stored after server verification with Google Play.

Data is used to enforce requested rules, verify pauses, prevent abuse, manage accounts, restore Premium, maintain security, and meet legal duties. Providers are Supabase, OpenAI, Google, and Cloudflare. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Photos exist only for the evaluation. Before production, configure a 90-day cleanup job for challenge/usage records. Purchase records are retained as needed for restoration and legal obligations. “Delete account and data” deletes linked UtilLock data; canceling a Google Play subscription is separate.

You may revoke Android permissions, stop the VPN, or delete your account in Profile. Contact `[PRIVACY EMAIL]` for applicable access, correction, deletion, objection, or portability rights. UtilLock is intended for people aged 16 or older.
